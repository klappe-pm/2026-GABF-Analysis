# bc-hop-farms

BC Hop Farms is recorded here from the sources listed below, and only as those sources state it. A blank field means no source checked states it.

## location

Woodburn, Marion County, OR. The county is derived: looked up from the city, not stated by the farm.

## ownership-and-history

- Family or ownership: Davidson family (Brandon Davidson leads; Bruce, Bill and Charlie Davidson named as owners of 3D Farm and BC Hop Farm Ltd.)
- Proprietary variety role: Strata grower
