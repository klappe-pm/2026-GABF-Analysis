# coleman-agriculture

Coleman Agriculture is recorded here from the sources listed below, and only as those sources state it. A blank field means no source checked states it.

## location

St. Paul, Marion County, OR. County is a geographic lookup from the city, not a claim by the farm.

## ownership-and-history

- Family or ownership: Coleman family
- Founded: mid-1800s (family arrived in Oregon 1847)
- Generation: seventh generation (Indie Hops and VinePair); a 2018 post says sixth
- Acreage: over 8,000 acres in the Willamette Valley (Yakima Chief page, search summary)

## supply-chain

- Affiliation as published: Yakima Chief Hops grower-owner (joined 2019); primary grower for Indie Hops
- Listed as a source farm of Yakima Chief Hops in the merchants file
- Listed as a source farm of Indie Hops in the merchants file

## notes

Indie Hops says five farms across the Willamette Valley and Central Oregon; also grows hazelnuts, seed crops and vegetables. Yakima Chief page reported as saying 24 distinct hop varieties and over 2 million pounds a year (search summary); a 2018 post says 22 varieties.
