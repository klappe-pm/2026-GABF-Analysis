# coleman-agriculture

Coleman Agriculture is recorded here from the sources listed below, and only as those sources state it. A blank field means no source checked states it.

## location

St. Paul, Marion County, OR. The county is derived: looked up from the city, not stated by the farm.

## ownership-and-history

- Family or ownership: Ben, Jen, Tom and Melissa Coleman
- Founded: 1847
- Generation: six generations (Yakima Chief page); seventh generation (Indie Hops and VinePair)
- Acreage: more than 2,000 acres of hops harvested; over 8,000 acres managed in the Willamette Valley (Yakima Chief page)
- Certifications: Partners with Salmon Safe (Yakima Chief page)

## supply-chain

- Affiliation as published: Yakima Chief Hops grower-owner (joined 2019); primary grower for Indie Hops
- Listed as a source farm of Yakima Chief Hops in the merchants file
- Listed as a source farm of Indie Hops in the merchants file

## notes

Archived Yakima Chief page: produces more than 2 million pounds a year, including 24 distinct hop varieties; also hazelnuts, grass seed, green beans and wheat. Indie Hops says five farms across the Willamette Valley and Central Oregon. Coleman Farms Inc, Coleman Ranches and Coleman Greenleaf appear separately on the YCR roster and the Oregon Hop Commission board and are not stated to be the same entity.
