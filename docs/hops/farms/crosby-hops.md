# crosby-hops

Crosby Hop Farm (Crosby Hops) is recorded here from the sources listed below, and only as those sources state it. A blank field means no source checked states it.

## location

Woodburn, Marion County, OR. County is a geographic lookup from the city, not a claim by the farm.

## ownership-and-history

- Family or ownership: Crosby family (Blake Crosby fifth generation)
- Founded: about 1900 (Albert Crosby planted first hops)
- Generation: fifth generation
- Acreage: over 1,000 acres of Estate Grown hops
- Certifications: B Corp; Salmon-Safe; SQF; Global G.A.P.
- Direct sales: Brewer portals for the US, Canada and EU suggest direct sales
- Proprietary variety role: Strata licensee; Comet Estate crop noted by a trade newsletter

## supply-chain

- Affiliation as published: Self: grower, processor and merchant; licensed to grow Strata for Indie Hops
- Listed as a source farm of Crosby Hops in the merchants file
- Listed as a source farm of Indie Hops in the merchants file

Headquarters 18564 Arbor Grove Rd NE, Woodburn. Site names no hop varieties.
