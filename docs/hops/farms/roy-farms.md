# roy-farms

Roy Farms Inc is recorded here from the sources listed below, and only as those sources state it. A blank field means no source checked states it.

## location

Yakima, Yakima County, WA. The county is derived: looked up from the city, not stated by the farm.

## ownership-and-history

- Family or ownership: Family-owned (Mike Roy featured)
- Certifications: Salmon-Safe and organic (Oregon Tilth episode, 2016)
- Direct sales: Licensed to sell Strata to US breweries (Indie Hops)
- Proprietary variety role: Strata licensee

## varieties-as-published

| variety | year | source | note | confirmed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strata | current | https://www.indiehops.com/growers | Indie Hops says Roy Farms is licensed to grow process and sell Strata to US breweries. | yes |

## supply-chain

- Affiliation as published: Roy Farms Inc. Named by Indie Hops as licensed to grow, process and sell Strata to US breweries. Named with Green Acre Farms and Wyckoff Farms as partners in the Association for the Development of Hop Agronomy.; on the YCR roster as Roy Farms Inc
- Listed as a source farm of Yakima Chief Ranches in the merchants file
- Listed as a source farm of Indie Hops in the merchants file
- Listed as a source farm of Association for the Development of Hop Agronomy in the merchants file

## notes

Oregon Tilth gives Yakima, WA. Indie Hops places it in the Yakima Valley. Its own site failed on an expired certificate. Not on the October 2026 archived Yakima Chief growers page. Hop Breeding Company membership is not stated by any page read.
