# gooding-farms

Gooding Farms is recorded here from the sources listed below, and only as those sources state it. A blank field means no source checked states it.

## location

Canyon County, ID.

## ownership-and-history

- Family or ownership: Godina brothers in management; Diane Gooding chairs the Idaho Hop Commission
- Founded: 1895
- Acreage: over 750 acres of hops
- Direct sales: The site's navigation lists "Order Hops"
- Proprietary variety role: Idaho Gem™ Brand '2012G01' Variety (proprietary per the farm's site)

## varieties-as-published

| variety | year | source | note | confirmed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Idaho Gem™ Brand '2012G01' Variety | current | https://www.goodingfarms.com/ | The only named variety; the site says 12 varieties are grown. | yes |

## supply-chain

- Affiliation as published: On the YCR roster as an Idaho grower; two Gooding Farms entries (grower numbers 105 and 122) on the 2026 Idaho grower list
- Listed as a source farm of Yakima Chief Ranches in the merchants file

## notes

Site says 12 varieties; only Idaho Gem is named. A search summary reported Parma as the location; the Idaho list gives Canyon County only, so the city is left blank.
