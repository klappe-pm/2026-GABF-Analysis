# goschie-farms

Goschie Farms is recorded here from the sources listed below, and only as those sources state it. A blank field means no source checked states it.

## location

Silverton, Marion County, OR. County is a geographic lookup from the city, not a claim by the farm.

## ownership-and-history

- Family or ownership: Goschie family (Goschie Farms Inc.; Gayle Goschie is described as a fourth generation farmer and third generation hop grower)
- Founded: 1885
- Generation: fourth generation farmer (Gayle Goschie)
- Certifications: Barley grown for Goschie Promise malt is Salmon Safe (Admiral Maltings); no hop certification stated
- Direct sales: Not described
