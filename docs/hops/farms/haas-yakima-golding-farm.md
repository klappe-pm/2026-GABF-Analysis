# haas-yakima-golding-farm

HAAS Yakima Golding Farm is recorded here from the sources listed below, and only as those sources state it. A blank field means no source checked states it.

## location

Toppenish and Wenas, WA. County is a geographic lookup from the city, not a claim by the farm.

## ownership-and-history

- Family or ownership: John I. Haas Inc.
- Founded: 1931 (Haas bought a Yakima farm)
- Acreage: over 1,800 acres of trellised fields (company site); Toppenish and Wenas farms well over 2,000 acres (later statement)
- Certifications: Global GAP
- Proprietary variety role: Haas is a Hop Breeding Company partner.

## varieties-as-published

No variety list for this farm was found in the sources checked.

## supply-chain

- Affiliation as published: John I. Haas Inc. (owner)
- Listed as a source farm of John I. Haas Inc. in the merchants file

## notes

Grows 12 commercial varieties and 10 to 12 experimental varieties depending on season (Global GAP article, undated). Wenas operation started September 2024. Haas history page says it was once the largest private hop grower with 25 farms and almost 4,000 acres.
