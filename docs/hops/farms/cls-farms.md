# cls-farms

CLS Farms is recorded here from the sources listed below, and only as those sources state it. A blank field means no source checked states it.

## location

Moxee, Yakima County, WA. The county is derived: looked up from the city, not stated by the farm.

## ownership-and-history

- Family or ownership: Family farm (owners not named on its site; Hopsteiner coverage names co-owner Shelley Desmarais)
- Generation: fifth generation
- Acreage: over 1400 acres of hop fields stretching from Wenas to Toppenish (2020 crop report)
- Certifications: GLOBALG.A.P.; organic for apples and soft fruit (fruit only)
- Direct sales: Site mentions custom hop blends and plants and farm tours; direct hop sales not described
- Proprietary variety role: Credited by the farm's site with proprietary El Dorado, Medusa and Zappa. Hopsteiner licensee for four varieties.

## supply-chain

- Affiliation as published: Independent grower. Licensed by Hopsteiner in 2022 to grow and market Sultana, Lotus, Calypso and Altus. Site names no merchant.
- Listed as a source farm of Hopsteiner in the merchants file
- Listed as a source farm of Yakima Valley Hops in the merchants file

## notes

Address 7810 Robillard Road, Moxee. Also grows cherries, apricots, peaches, nectarines, plums, pears and apples. Not confirmed from a source as a Hop Breeding Company member farm.
