# riwaka

Riwaka is a hop variety from New Zealand, proprietary, from Plant & Food Research hop breeding program (New Zealand). It was released in 1997. Its published alpha acid range is 4.5 to 6.5 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Trademark | Riwaka (trademark, per Hopsteiner) |

| Year released | 1997 |
| Parentage | A cross of a New Zealand breeding selection and Saaz (Charles Faram) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | low (below 8 percent) |

Also published as: Riwaka Cryo.
