# riwaka

Riwaka is a hop variety from New Zealand, proprietary, from Plant & Food Research hop breeding program (New Zealand). It was released in 1997. Its published alpha acid range is 4.5 to 6.5 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | New Zealand |
| Breeder or owner program | Plant & Food Research hop breeding program (New Zealand) |
| Trademark | Riwaka (trademark, per Hopsteiner) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 1997 |
| Parentage | A cross of a New Zealand breeding selection and Saaz (Charles Faram) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | low (below 8 percent) |

Also published as: Riwaka Cryo.

## chemistry

Ranges are as published by the breeder or supplier named under sources; a single value means the source printed one figure.

| Measure | Published | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| alpha acids | 4.5 to 6.5 | percent |
| beta acids | 4 to 5 | percent |
| cohumulone | 30 to 34 | percent of alpha acids |
| total oil | 1 to 1.5 | ml per 100 g |
| myrcene | 65 to 70 | percent of total oil |
| humulene | 5 to 10 | percent of total oil |
| caryophyllene | not published | percent of total oil |
| farnesene | 1 (single value) | percent of total oil |

Total oil is from Hopsteiner (1.0 to 1.5); Charles Faram prints 10.2 to 10.8 ml per 100 g, which is not plausible and is not recorded. Oil composition is from Charles Faram.

## aroma

As published by Hopsteiner data sheet; Charles Faram: "citrus and passionfruit": "Passion fruit, citrus, grapefruit"

## acreage

This variety is not grown commercially in the United States as far as the sources checked show, so US acreage does not apply.
