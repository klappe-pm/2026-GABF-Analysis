# terraflux

TerraFlux is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hop Breeding Company (joint venture of John I. Haas and Yakima Chief Ranches). Its published alpha acid range is 14.4 to 22.6 percent.

## identity

|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Hop Breeding Company (joint venture of John I. Haas and Yakima Chief Ranches) |
| Trademark | TerraFlux (trademark of Hop Breeding Company, per the John I. Haas sheet) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | not published |

| Published use | bittering (bittering (Hop Breeding Company: first high alpha bittering hop it released)) |
| Alpha class | high (14 percent and above) |

Also published as: TerraFlux HBC 682, HBC 682.

## chemistry

NASS lists this variety as HBC 682 with no trademark mark; the Hop Breeding Company later named it TerraFlux.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "citrus, earthy, herbal, stone fruit, and woody"
