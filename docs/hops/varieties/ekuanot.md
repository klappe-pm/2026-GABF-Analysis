# ekuanot

Ekuanot is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hop Breeding Company (joint venture of John I. Haas and Yakima Chief Ranches). It was released in 2014. Its published alpha acid range is 14.5 to 15.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Green pepper, papaya, lime, apple blossom"
