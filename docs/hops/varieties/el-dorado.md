# el-dorado

El Dorado is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from CLS Farms, LLC (Moxee, Washington). It was released in 2010. Its published alpha acid range is 13 to 17 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Pear, watermelon, apricot, wintergreen, mandarin"
