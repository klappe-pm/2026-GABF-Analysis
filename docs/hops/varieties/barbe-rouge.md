# barbe-rouge

Barbe Rouge is a hop variety from France, proprietary, from Comptoir Agricole breeding program (R&D Alsace). Its published alpha acid range is 7.5 to 9.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by Comptoir Agricole technical data: "Red fruits, strawberry, bubble gum"

## acreage

This variety is not grown commercially in the United States as far as the sources checked show, so US acreage does not apply.

## gaps

- Identity, year_released: The sheet is dated September 2017 and gives no release year.
- Identity, parentage: The Comptoir Agricole sheet publishes no parentage.
- Chemistry, caryophyllene, farnesene not published. Caryophyllene and farnesene shares: the sheet gives farnesene in mg per 100 g (0 to 3), not as a share of oil, and a humulene to caryophyllene ratio (3 to 3.3).
