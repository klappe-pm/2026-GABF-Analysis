# hallertauer-tradition

Hallertauer Tradition is a hop variety from Germany, public, from Hüll Hop Research Center (Germany). It was released in 1993. Its published alpha acid range is 4 to 7 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Apricot, peach, cassis, orange."
