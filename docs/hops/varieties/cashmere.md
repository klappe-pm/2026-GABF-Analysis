# cashmere

Cashmere is a hop variety from the United States, public, from Washington State University and USDA public breeding program. It was released in 2013. Its published alpha acid range is 7.7 to 9.1 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Lemon, lime, ripe melon, stone fruit, thyme."
