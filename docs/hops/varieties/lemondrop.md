# lemondrop

Lemondrop is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hopsteiner breeding program. Its published alpha acid range is 5 to 7 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Hopsteiner breeding program |
| Trademark | Lemondrop (trademark, per Hopsteiner) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | not published |
| Parentage | Aroma type hop from a cross between Cascade and USDA 19058 male (Hopsteiner experimental hop 01210) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | low (below 8 percent) |

Also published as: Lemon Drop, Lemondrop Hopsteiner 01210.

## chemistry

Ranges are as published by the breeder or supplier named under sources; a single value means the source printed one figure.

| Measure | Published | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| alpha acids | 5 to 7 | percent |
| beta acids | 4 to 6 | percent |
| cohumulone | 28 to 34 | percent of alpha acids |
| total oil | 1.5 to 2 | ml per 100 g |
| myrcene | not published | percent of total oil |
| humulene | not published | percent of total oil |
| caryophyllene | not published | percent of total oil |
| farnesene | 6 to 7 | percent of total oil |

## aroma

As published by Hopsteiner data sheet: "lemon, green tea, light melon"

## acreage

NASS names no line for this variety in the National Hop Report, so no state acreage is published for it.

## gaps

- Identity, year_released: The Hopsteiner data sheet publishes no release year.
- Chemistry, myrcene, humulene, caryophyllene not published. Myrcene, humulene and caryophyllene shares: the Hopsteiner sheet lists only the caryophyllene to humulene ratio (0.56 to 0.58), farnesene and linalool.
