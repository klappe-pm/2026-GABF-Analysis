# callista

Callista is a hop variety from Germany, public, from Hüll Hop Research Center (Germany). Its published alpha acid range is 2 to 5 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Strawberry, orange, passion fruit, pear, caramel"

## acreage

This variety is not grown commercially in the United States as far as the sources checked show, so US acreage does not apply.
