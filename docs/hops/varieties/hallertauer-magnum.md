# hallertauer-magnum

Hallertauer Magnum is a hop variety from Germany, public, from Hüll Hop Research Center (Germany). It was released in 1980. Its published alpha acid range is 11 to 16 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Lemon, green pepper, spearmint, apple."
