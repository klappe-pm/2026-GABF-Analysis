# fuggle

Fuggle is a hop variety from England, public, from English seedling selection. It was released in 1875. Its published alpha acid range is 3 to 6 percent.

Also published as: Fuggles.

Ranges are as published by the breeder or supplier named under sources; a single value means the source printed one figure.

| humulene | 30 to 35 | percent of total oil |
| caryophyllene | not published | percent of total oil |
| farnesene | 5 to 6 | percent of total oil |

Myrcene and humulene shares are from the Comptoir Agricole sheet (French grown Fuggle); its alpha range is 4.1 to 5.5 and its total oil 0.5 to 1 ml per 100 g, which differ from Hopsteiner.

## aroma

As published by Hopsteiner data sheet: "mild, grassy, mint, earthy"

## acreage

This variety is not grown commercially in the United States as far as the sources checked show, so US acreage does not apply.

## gaps

- Chemistry, caryophyllene not published. Caryophyllene share: the Hopsteiner sheet lists only the caryophyllene to humulene ratio (above 0.30) and the Comptoir Agricole sheet lists a humulene to caryophyllene ratio of 2.8 to 3.5, not a caryophyllene share.
