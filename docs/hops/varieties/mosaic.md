# mosaic

Mosaic is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hop Breeding Company (joint venture of John I. Haas and Yakima Chief Ranches). It was released in 2012. Its published alpha acid range is 11.5 to 13.5 percent.

Also published as: Mosaic HBC 369, HBC 369, HBC369, Mosaic Brand, Mosaic LUPOMAX, Mosaic Incognito.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Tangerine, lime, peach, passion fruit, blueberry"
