# nectaron

Nectaron is a hop variety from New Zealand, proprietary, from New Zealand Hops Ltd with Plant & Food Research. It was released in 2020. Its published alpha acid range is 10.5 to 11.5 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | New Zealand |
| Breeder or owner program | New Zealand Hops Ltd with Plant & Food Research |
| Trademark | Nectaron (registered, per Charles Faram) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2020 |
| Parentage | Full sister to Waimea; named for breeder Dr. Ron Beatson (Charles Faram) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | medium (8 to 14 percent) |

Also published as: Nectaron NZ Hops.

Ranges are as published by the breeder or supplier named under sources; a single value means the source printed one figure.

## aroma

As published by Charles Faram: "grapefruit, ripe tropical fruit, and juicy peach"

## acreage

This variety is not grown commercially in the United States as far as the sources checked show, so US acreage does not apply.
