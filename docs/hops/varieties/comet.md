# comet

Comet is a hop variety from the United States, public, from USDA public breeding program. It was released in 1975. Its published alpha acid range is 9.4 to 12.4 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Mandarin, lemongrass, black currant, apricot, pineapple."
