# talus

Talus is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hop Breeding Company (joint venture of John I. Haas and Yakima Chief Ranches). It was released in 2020. Its published alpha acid range is 8.1 to 9.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Pink Grapefruit, Dried Roses, Pine, Resin"
