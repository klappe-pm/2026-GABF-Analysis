# nugget

Nugget is a hop variety from the United States, public, from USDA public breeding program at Oregon State University. It was released in 1984. Its published alpha acid range is 11.5 to 14 percent.

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Fresh-crushed herbs, grapefruit zest, stone fruit, pineapple, resin"
