# rakau

Rakau is a hop variety from New Zealand, proprietary, from HortResearch (now Plant & Food Research). It was released in 2007. Its published alpha acid range is 10 to 12 percent.

Also published as: Rakau Cryo, AlphAroma.

Humulene and farnesene are single published values. Classed proprietary on the evidence that a New Zealand institute released it under a brand name; no trademark text was read.

## aroma

As published by Charles Faram, which also quotes "the whole orchard": "a range of stone fruit aromatics"

## acreage

This variety is not grown commercially in the United States as far as the sources checked show, so US acreage does not apply.
