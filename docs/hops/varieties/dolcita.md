# dolcita

Dolcita is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hop Breeding Company (joint venture of John I. Haas and Yakima Chief Ranches). Its published alpha acid range is 10 to 12 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Hop Breeding Company (joint venture of John I. Haas and Yakima Chief Ranches) |
| Trademark | Dolcita (registered, per the Hop Breeding Company) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | not published |
| Parentage | A 2016 cross between two HBC developmental lines (John I. Haas sheet) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | medium (8 to 14 percent) |

Also published as: Dolcita HBC 1019, HBC 1019, Dolcita DynaBoost.

Oil components on the sheet are single values, not ranges. The Hop Breeding Company lists alpha as 7.5 to 12.9 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Peach, pineapple, orange, crème-caramel"

## acreage

NASS names no line for this variety in the National Hop Report, so no state acreage is published for it.

## gaps

- Identity, year_released: The sheet says the variety is the result of a 2016 cross; no release year is published on the sheet or the Hop Breeding Company page.
