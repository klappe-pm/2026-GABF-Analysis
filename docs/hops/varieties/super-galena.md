# super-galena

Super Galena is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hopsteiner breeding program. Its published alpha acid range is 13 to 16 percent.

## gaps

- Identity, year_released: The Hopsteiner data sheet publishes no release year.
- Chemistry, myrcene, humulene, caryophyllene not published. Myrcene, humulene and caryophyllene shares: the Hopsteiner sheet lists only the caryophyllene to humulene ratio (0.38 to 0.47) and linalool.
