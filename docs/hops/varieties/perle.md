# perle

Perle is a hop variety from Germany, with proprietary status not published, from German breeding program (John I. Haas sheet does not name the institute). It was released in 1978. Its published alpha acid range is 4 to 9 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Marjoram, peppermint, lime, pear."
