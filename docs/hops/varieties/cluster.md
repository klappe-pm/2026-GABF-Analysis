# cluster

Cluster is a hop variety from the United States, public, from Long established U.S. variety; rootstock origin uncertain. Its published alpha acid range is 5.5 to 8.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Lilac, apricot, lemon"
