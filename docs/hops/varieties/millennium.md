# millennium

Millennium is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from John I. Haas breeding program. It was released in 2000. Its published alpha acid range is 14.5 to 16.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Mild, somewhat pungent"

## acreage

NASS names no line for this variety in the National Hop Report, so no state acreage is published for it.
