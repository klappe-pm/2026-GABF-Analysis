# hallertauer-mittelfruh

Hallertauer Mittelfruh is a hop variety from Germany, public, from German landrace. Its published alpha acid range is 3 to 5.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Sage, rosemary, lavender, pine"
