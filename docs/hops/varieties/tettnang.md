# tettnang

Tettnang is a hop variety from Germany, public, from Traditional Lake Constance landrace of the Saaz group. Its published alpha acid range is 2.5 to 5.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Lily of the valley, cognac, chocolate, bergamot."
