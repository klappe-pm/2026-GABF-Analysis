# strata

Strata is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Indie Hops (Oregon). It was released in 2009. Its published alpha acid range is 12.1 (single value) percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Indie Hops (Oregon) |
| Trademark | Strata (trademark, per the John I. Haas sheet; registered, per NASS) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2009 |
| Parentage | Open pollination of a Perle mother in an Oregon experimental hop yard (formerly X-331) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | medium (8 to 14 percent) |

Also published as: Strata OR 91331, OR 91331, X-331, Strata Cryo.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Melon, bubblegum, strawberries, fresh-rubbed sage"
