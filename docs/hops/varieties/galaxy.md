# galaxy

Galaxy is a hop variety from Australia, proprietary, from Hop Products Australia (Tasmania and Victoria). It was released in 2009. Its published alpha acid range is 15.9 to 19.8 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | Australia |
| Breeder or owner program | Hop Products Australia (Tasmania and Victoria) |
| Trademark | Galaxy (registered, per Hop Products Australia) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2009 (Created in 1994 and commercialised in 2009 (Hop Products Australia).) |
| Parentage | Cross pollination of high alpha Australian and Perle hops (Hop Products Australia, c.v. 94-203-008) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | high (14 percent and above) |

Also published as: Galaxy Cryo.

## aroma

As published by Hop Products Australia data sheet: "Passionfruit, Peach, Orange, Lemon"
