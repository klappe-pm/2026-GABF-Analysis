# vista

Vista is a hop variety from the United States, public, from USDA Agricultural Research Service public breeding program (bred by Dr. John Henning). It was released in 2021. Its published alpha acid range is 11 to 12 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |

| Trademark | none found |
| Proprietary or public | public |
| Year released | 2021 (Charles Faram: named for commercial release in 2021 after development since 2006; formerly USDA 074.) |
| Parentage | not published |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | medium (8 to 14 percent) |

Also published as: USDA 074, USDA-074.

## chemistry

Ranges are as published by the breeder or supplier named under sources; a single value means the source printed one figure.

| humulene | not published | percent of total oil |
| caryophyllene | not published | percent of total oil |
| farnesene | not published | percent of total oil |

Charles Faram publishes alpha 10 to 12, beta 4.5 to 5 and total oil 2 to 2.4 ml per 100 g. The Yakima Valley Hops figures are recorded.

## aroma

As published by Yakima Valley Hops: "Tropical Fruit, Tangerine, Melon, Pear, Green Tea"

## acreage

NASS names no line for this variety in the National Hop Report, so no state acreage is published for it.

## gaps

- Identity, parentage: Neither supplier publishes parentage.
- Chemistry, cohumulone, myrcene, humulene, caryophyllene, farnesene not published. Cohumulone and oil components: Yakima Valley Hops lists none; Charles Faram lists cohumulone 30 percent and humulene 20 percent but prints myrcene as "0.05", which cannot be read as a percentage.
