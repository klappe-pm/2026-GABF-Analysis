# eclipse

Eclipse is a hop variety from Australia, proprietary, from Hop Products Australia (Tasmania and Victoria). It was released in 2020. Its published alpha acid range is 17.4 to 19.1 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | Australia |
| Breeder or owner program | Hop Products Australia (Tasmania and Victoria) |
| Trademark | Eclipse (registered, per Hop Products Australia) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2020 (Created in 2004 and commercialised in 2020 (Hop Products Australia).) |
| Parentage | Cross pollination of high alpha Australian and North American hops, with Fuggle, Brewer's Gold, Comet and Pride of Ringwood in earlier generations; half sister of Galaxy |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | high (14 percent and above) |

Also published as: Eclipse 04-337-016.
