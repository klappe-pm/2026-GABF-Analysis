# spalter-select

Spalter Select is a hop variety from Germany, public, from Hüll Hop Research Center breeding stock (Germany). Its published alpha acid range is 3 to 6.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Citrus, woody, black tea, chamomile blossoms"

## acreage

This variety is not grown commercially in the United States as far as the sources checked show, so US acreage does not apply.
