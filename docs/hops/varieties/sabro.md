# sabro

Sabro is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hop Breeding Company (joint venture of John I. Haas and Yakima Chief Ranches). It was released in 2018. Its published alpha acid range is 12 to 16 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Tangerine, tropical fruit, stone fruit, coconut."
