# motueka

Motueka is a hop variety from New Zealand, proprietary, from HortResearch (now Plant & Food Research) hop breeding program. Its published alpha acid range is 6.5 to 7.5 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | New Zealand |
| Breeder or owner program | HortResearch (now Plant & Food Research) hop breeding program |
| Trademark | Motueka (trademark, per the John I. Haas sheet) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | not published |
| Parentage | Triploid cross of Saaz and a New Zealand breeding selection (John I. Haas sheet) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | low (below 8 percent) |

Also published as: Motueka Cryo.

## chemistry

Ranges are as published by the breeder or supplier named under sources; a single value means the source printed one figure.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Lime Zest, Stone Fruits, Lemongrass, Banana, Basil"

## acreage

This variety is not grown commercially in the United States as far as the sources checked show, so US acreage does not apply.
