# hersbrucker

Hersbrucker is a hop variety from Germany, public, from Traditional German variety from the Hersbrucker growing area. Its published alpha acid range is 1.5 to 4 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Lemon balm, orange, black tea, marjoram"

## acreage

This variety is not grown commercially in the United States as far as the sources checked show, so US acreage does not apply.
