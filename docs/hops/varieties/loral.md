# loral

Loral is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hop Breeding Company (joint venture of John I. Haas and Yakima Chief Ranches). It was released in 2016. Its published alpha acid range is 10 to 12 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Violet, lily, fennel frond, lemon"
