# simcoe

Simcoe is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Yakima Chief Ranches. It was released in 2000. Its published alpha acid range is 12 to 14 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Yakima Chief Ranches |
| Trademark | Simcoe (registered, per NASS and the John I. Haas sheet) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2000 (Released and trademarked in 2000 (John I. Haas sheet).) |
| Parentage | Open pollination |
| Published use | dual (dual) |
| Alpha class | medium (8 to 14 percent) |

Also published as: Simcoe YCR 14, YCR 14, YCR14, Simcoe Brand, Simcoe Cryo, Simcoe LUPOMAX.

## aroma
