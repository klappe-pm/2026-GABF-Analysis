# columbus-tomahawk-zeus

Columbus/Tomahawk/Zeus is a hop group from the United States, proprietary, from Not stated on the John I. Haas sheet. Its published alpha acid range is 15 to 17 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Not stated on the John I. Haas sheet |
| Trademark | Columbus/Tomahawk and Zeus carry a registered mark in the NASS table |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | not published |
| Parentage | Share the same female parent as Nugget (John I. Haas sheet) |
| Published use | bittering (beer bittering (John I. Haas sheet)) |
| Alpha class | high (14 percent and above) |

Also published as: CTZ, Columbus Tomahawk Zeus, Tomahawk, Zeus.

## chemistry

Ranges are as published by the breeder or supplier named under sources; a single value means the source printed one figure.

| Measure | Published | Unit |

| alpha acids | 15 to 17 | percent |
| beta acids | 4.5 to 5 | percent |

| total oil | 2.5 to 3.5 | ml per 100 g |

| humulene | 12 to 18 | percent of total oil |
| caryophyllene | 9 to 11 | percent of total oil |
| farnesene | up to 1 | percent of total oil |

NASS reports Columbus, Tomahawk and Zeus together as one line, so the acreage applies to the group, not to Columbus alone.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Lemon, black pepper, green onion, mango."
