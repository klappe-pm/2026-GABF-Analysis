# helios

Helios is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hopsteiner breeding program. Its published alpha acid range is 15.5 to 18.5 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Hopsteiner breeding program |
| Trademark | Helios (trademark, per NASS and Hopsteiner) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | not published |
| Parentage | not published |
| Published use | bittering (super alpha with clean bitterness (Hopsteiner)) |
| Alpha class | high (14 percent and above) |

Also published as: Helios HS15619, HS15619.

## aroma

As published by Hopsteiner data sheet (the sheet lists this as its aroma specification): "Super Alpha with clean bitterness"

## gaps

- Identity, year_released: not published in the sources checked (`https://hopsteiner.us/variety-data-sheets/Helios`)
- Identity, parentage: not published in the sources checked (`https://hopsteiner.us/variety-data-sheets/Helios`)

- Chemistry, total oil, myrcene, humulene, caryophyllene, farnesene not published. Total oil and oil components: the Hopsteiner sheet prints a total oil range that runs from 1.5 down to 0.0 and a caryophyllene to humulene ratio from 0.04 up to 0.55, which cannot be read as published ranges, so they are not recorded.
