# hallertau-blanc

Hallertau Blanc is a hop variety from Germany, public, from Hüll Hop Research Center (Germany). It was released in 2012. Its published alpha acid range is 9 to 11 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "White grapes, cassis, lemongrass, grapefruit."
