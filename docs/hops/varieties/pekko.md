# pekko

Pekko is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Association for the Development of Hop Agronomy (ADHA), Yakima Valley. Its published alpha acid range is 13 to 16 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |

| Trademark | Pekko (registered, per NASS and the John I. Haas sheet) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | not published |
| Parentage | Mother ADHA 538, open pollination (John I. Haas sheet) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | medium (8 to 14 percent) |

Also published as: Pekko ADHA 871, ADHA 871, ADHA871.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Lemongrass, eucalyptus, sage, pineapple, banana, lilac, chamomile"
