# idaho-7

Idaho 7 is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Jackson Hop Farm (Wilder, Idaho). It was released in 2015. Its published alpha acid range is 13 to 15 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Jackson Hop Farm (Wilder, Idaho) |
| Trademark | Idaho 7 (trademark, per the John I. Haas sheet; registered, per NASS) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2015 (Originally known as 007 The Golden Hop (John I. Haas sheet).) |
| Parentage | not published |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | medium (8 to 14 percent) |

Also published as: Idaho-7, Idaho7, 007 The Golden Hop.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Guava, apricot, pine resin, lemon, orange pith."
