# elani

Elani is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Yakima Quality Hops. It was released in 2021. Its published alpha acid range is 4 to 7 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Yakima Quality Hops |
| Trademark | Elani (registered, per NASS and Yakima Valley Hops) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2021 (Charles Faram states commercial release in 2021; Yakima Valley Hops states 2022.) |
| Parentage | A found variety, discovered growing wild in a river valley in Idaho (Charles Faram) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | low (below 8 percent) |

Also published as: Elani YQH 1320, YQH 1320, YQH-1320.

The two suppliers disagree on alpha: Yakima Valley Hops lists 4.0 to 7.0 percent, Charles Faram 9 to 11 percent (beta 4 to 7, cohumulone 30 to 34, total oil 1.3 to 2.0 ml per 100 g). The Yakima Valley Hops figures are recorded.

## aroma

As published by Yakima Valley Hops: "Pineapple, guava, lime, white peach, orange zest"
