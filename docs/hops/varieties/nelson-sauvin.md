# nelson-sauvin

Nelson Sauvin is a hop variety from New Zealand, proprietary, from HortResearch (now Plant & Food Research) hop breeding program. It was released in 2000. Its published alpha acid range is 12 to 13 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | New Zealand |

| Trademark | Nelson Sauvin (trademark, per the John I. Haas sheet) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2000 |
| Parentage | Triploid variety bred from the New Zealand variety Smoothcone (John I. Haas sheet) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | medium (8 to 14 percent) |

Also published as: Nelson, Nelson Sauvin Cryo, Nelson Cryo, Nelson Sauvin NZ.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "White wine, gooseberry, grapefruit, mango, lychee, melon"
