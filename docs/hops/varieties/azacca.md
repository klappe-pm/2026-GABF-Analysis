# azacca

Azacca is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Association for the Development of Hop Agronomy (ADHA), Yakima Valley. It was released in 2013. Its published alpha acid range is 14 to 16 percent.

|---|---|

| Trademark | Azacca (registered, per NASS and the John I. Haas sheet) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2013 |
| Parentage | Toyomidori crossed with ADHA 94/95 (Hopsteiner sheet) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | high (14 percent and above) |

Also published as: Azacca ADHA 483, ADHA 483, ADHA483.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Ripe mango, papaya, pineapple, Valencia orange, grapefruit, pine resin, fresh grass"
