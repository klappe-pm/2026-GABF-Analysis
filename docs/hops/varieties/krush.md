# krush

Krush is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hop Breeding Company (joint venture of John I. Haas and Yakima Chief Ranches). Its published alpha acid range is 10.1 to 14.1 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "ripe tropical fruits like mango and guava as well as rich stone fruits like peach"
