# citra

Citra is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hop Breeding Company (joint venture of John I. Haas and Yakima Chief Ranches). It was released in 2007. Its published alpha acid range is 11 to 13 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |

| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2007 (The Hop Breeding Company states 2007; the John I. Haas sheet states 2008.) |

| Published use | aroma (aroma hop (Hop Breeding Company: "flavor-forward aroma hop")) |

Also published as: Citra HBC 394, HBC 394, HBC394, Citra Brand.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Mango, grapefruit flesh, lime zest."
