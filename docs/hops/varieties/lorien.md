# lorien

Lorien is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Indie Hops (Oregon). It was released in 2021. Its published alpha acid range is 4 to 5.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by Yakima Valley Hops, quoting Indie Hops: "Citrus zest, fresh melon, mixed wildflowers, a hint of cinnamon spice"

## acreage
