# lorien

Lorien is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Indie Hops (Oregon). It was released in 2021. Its published alpha acid range is 4 to 5.5 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Indie Hops (Oregon) |
| Trademark | Lórien (registered, per Yakima Valley Hops) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2021 |
| Parentage | Sterling female crossed with a German male |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | low (below 8 percent) |

Also published as: Lórien.

## chemistry

Ranges are as published by the breeder or supplier named under sources; a single value means the source printed one figure.

| Measure | Published | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| alpha acids | 4 to 5.5 | percent |
| beta acids | 7 to 9 | percent |
| cohumulone | 22.6 (single value) | percent of alpha acids |
| total oil | 1.8 to 2.2 | ml per 100 g |
| myrcene | 40 to 50 | percent of total oil |
| humulene | 19 to 25 | percent of total oil |
| caryophyllene | 2.6 to 5 | percent of total oil |
| farnesene | 2.7 to 4 | percent of total oil |

Yakima Valley Hops prints the beta acid upper bound with a missing digit (7.0 to "9."); it is recorded as 9. Cohumulone is a single published value.

## aroma

As published by Yakima Valley Hops, quoting Indie Hops: "Citrus zest, fresh melon, mixed wildflowers, a hint of cinnamon spice"

## acreage

NASS names no line for this variety in the National Hop Report, so no state acreage is published for it.
