# crystal

Crystal is a hop variety from the United States, public, from USDA public breeding program. It was released in 1993. Its published alpha acid range is 4 to 6 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Cedar, myrrh, chamomile, nutmeg."
