# saaz

Saaz is a hop variety from Czechia, public, from Czech landrace, cultivated into nine clones since 1952 (John I. Haas sheet). Its published alpha acid range is 2.5 to 4.5 percent.

Also published as: Czech Saaz, Saaz Czech, Zatec, Zatecky.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Bergamot, honey, woody, chamomile tea, spicy"
