# columbus

Columbus is a hop variety from the United States, with proprietary status not published, from Not stated on the John I. Haas sheet. Its published alpha acid range is 15 to 17 percent.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Not stated on the John I. Haas sheet |
| Trademark | none found |
| Proprietary or public | not published |
| Year released | not published |
| Parentage | Shares the same female parent as Nugget (John I. Haas sheet, for Columbus, Tomahawk and Zeus) |
| Published use | bittering (beer bittering (John I. Haas sheet: used almost exclusively for bittering)) |
| Alpha class | high (14 percent and above) |

Also published as: Columbus Cryo.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Lemon, black pepper, green onion, mango"

## acreage

NASS names no line for this variety in the National Hop Report, so no state acreage is published for it.
