# anchovy

Anchovy is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Segal Ranch (Yakima Valley, Washington). Its published alpha acid range is 12.6 (single value) percent.

| Trademark | none found |

| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | medium (8 to 14 percent) |

Also published as: Anchovy 24B-05, 24B-05.

## chemistry

Ranges are as published by the breeder or supplier named under sources; a single value means the source printed one figure.

| humulene | not published | percent of total oil |

Experimental variety; the figures are single values from one crop year. Yakima Valley Hops prints total oil as "1.61%"; it is recorded as ml per 100 g on the assumption that the unit was mislabeled.

## aroma

As published by Yakima Valley Hops: "Watermelon, Raspberry, Pine"

## acreage

NASS names no line for this variety in the National Hop Report, so no state acreage is published for it.

## gaps

- Identity, year_released: Yakima Valley Hops calls it a new experimental variety (2024 crop listed); no release year is published.
- Identity, parentage: Yakima Valley Hops lists the pedigree as experimental variety 24B-05 only.
- Chemistry, cohumulone, myrcene, humulene, caryophyllene, farnesene not published. Cohumulone and oil components: Yakima Valley Hops lists only alpha, beta, total oil and HSI (0.261).
