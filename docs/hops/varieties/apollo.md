# apollo

Apollo is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Hopsteiner breeding program. It was released in 2006. Its published alpha acid range is 15 to 19 percent.

|---|---|

| Trademark | Apollo (trademark, per NASS) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |

| Parentage | Zeus crossed with (98001 x USDA 19058m) (Hopsteiner); daughter of Zeus (John I. Haas) |
| Published use | bittering (bittering) |
| Alpha class | high (14 percent and above) |

Also published as: Apollo Hopsteiner 01051.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet; Hopsteiner lists "lime, grapefruit, pine": "Strong grapefruit note"
