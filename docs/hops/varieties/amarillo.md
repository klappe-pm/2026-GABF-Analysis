# amarillo

Amarillo is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Virgil Gamache Farms (Washington State). Its published alpha acid range is 8 to 11 percent.

|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Virgil Gamache Farms (Washington State) |
| Trademark | Amarillo (registered, per NASS and the John I. Haas sheet) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | not published |
| Parentage | Derived from open pollination (John I. Haas sheet) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | medium (8 to 14 percent) |

Also published as: Amarillo VGXP01, VGXP01, Amarillo Brand.
