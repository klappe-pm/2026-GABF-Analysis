# warrior

Warrior is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Yakima Chief Ranches. Its published alpha acid range is 15.5 to 18 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | United States |
| Breeder or owner program | Yakima Chief Ranches |
| Trademark | Warrior (registered, per NASS and the John I. Haas sheet) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | not published |
| Parentage | not published |
| Published use | bittering (high alpha variety (John I. Haas sheet)) |
| Alpha class | high (14 percent and above) |

Also published as: Warrior YCR 5, YCR 5.

## chemistry

Ranges are as published by the breeder or supplier named under sources; a single value means the source printed one figure.

| Measure | Published | Unit |

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Tangerine, honeysuckle, sweet basil"
