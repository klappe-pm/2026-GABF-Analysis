# saphir

Saphir is a hop variety from Germany, public, from Hüll Hop Research Center (Germany). It was released in 2002. Its published alpha acid range is 2 to 4.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Juniper, lemongrass, bergamot, strawberry, black tea"
