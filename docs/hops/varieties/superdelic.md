# superdelic

Superdelic is a hop variety from New Zealand, proprietary, from New Zealand Hops Ltd with Plant & Food Research. It was released in 2023. Its published alpha acid range is 9 to 10.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by Yakima Valley Hops, quoting the NZ Hops variety descriptors: "Citrus, berry fruit, tropical fruit, candy/lolly"
