# centennial

Centennial is a hop variety from the United States, public, from USDA public breeding program at Washington State University. It was released in 1990. Its published alpha acid range is 9.5 to 11.5 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Orange, lime, cherry, pine resin, orange blossom."
