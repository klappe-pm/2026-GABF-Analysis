# willamette

Willamette is a hop variety from the United States, public, from USDA public breeding program in Oregon. It was released in 1976. Its published alpha acid range is 4 to 6 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Cedar, incense, anise, marjoram."
