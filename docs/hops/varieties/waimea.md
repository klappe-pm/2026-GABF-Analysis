# waimea

Waimea is a hop variety from New Zealand, proprietary, from Plant & Food Research hop breeding program (New Zealand). It was released in 2012. Its published alpha acid range is 16 to 19 percent.

## identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Origin | New Zealand |
| Breeder or owner program | Plant & Food Research hop breeding program (New Zealand) |
| Trademark | Waimea (trademark symbol on the Charles Faram page) |
| Proprietary or public | proprietary |
| Year released | 2012 |
| Parentage | Parentage traces back to Cluster, Saaz and Fuggle (Charles Faram) |
| Published use | not published |
| Alpha class | high (14 percent and above) |

Also published as: Waimea Cryo.

## chemistry

Ranges are as published by the breeder or supplier named under sources; a single value means the source printed one figure.

| Measure | Published | Unit |

| beta acids | 7 to 9 | percent |
| cohumulone | 22 to 24 | percent of alpha acids |
| total oil | not published | ml per 100 g |
| myrcene | not published | percent of total oil |
| humulene | 9 to 10 | percent of total oil |
| caryophyllene | not published | percent of total oil |
| farnesene | 5 (single value) | percent of total oil |

Humulene and farnesene are as published (farnesene a single value).

## aroma

As published by Charles Faram: "an aroma abundant with fruity citrus and pine characters"

## acreage

This variety is not grown commercially in the United States as far as the sources checked show, so US acreage does not apply.
