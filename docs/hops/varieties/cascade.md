# cascade

Cascade is a hop variety from the United States, public, from USDA public breeding program at Oregon State University. It was released in 1972. Its published alpha acid range is 4.5 to 7 percent.

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Floral, grapefruit, pine resin"
