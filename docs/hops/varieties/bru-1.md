# bru-1

BRU-1 is a hop variety from the United States, proprietary, from Brulotte Farms (Toppenish, Washington). Its published alpha acid range is 13 to 15 percent.

## aroma

As published by John I. Haas variety sheet: "Pineapple, Pear, Apple, Fresh Cut Grass"

## acreage

NASS names no line for this variety in the National Hop Report, so no state acreage is published for it.
