Cucumber is the only ingredient named, through the brewery's style label and a Bend Source report calling it "a cucumber gose that tastes even better than Cucumber Crush did" ([Bend Source](https://www.bendsource.com/food-drink/gabf-is-down-with-upp/)). No hops, malts, yeast or salt are published.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Field Beer asks for vegetable aroma and flavor, subtle to intense, that is not masked by hops, with IBU and ABV varying with the base style. The brewery's cucumber label fits the vegetable requirement.
