## ingredients

The brewery describes Styrian Golding as herbaceous and earthy with lemongrass, tea and herbal aroma, and US Tettnanger as woody, floral and bergamot. Its malt note says: "In addition to our base two row malt we use a variety of darker malts". DBA goes through primary fermentation with the house ale yeast in a Union system modeled on Burton Unions, using medium and medium plus toasted American oak barrels meant to add vanilla, caramel and woody flavor ([Firestone Walker: DBA](https://www.firestonebeer.com/beer/dba/)).

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Classic English-Style Pale Ale call for a gold to copper ale with floral, earthy English-style hops and medium body, at 4.4 to 5.3 percent ABV, 20 to 40 IBU and 5 to 12 SRM. The published 5% ABV and 30 IBU fit; if the color figure of 16.5 is SRM it would be darker than the guideline.
