## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) page lists no style named International Dark Lager; it does list Mexican-Style Dark Lager, and its European-Style Dark Lager entry calls for 4.8 to 5.3 percent ABV and 20 to 35 IBU with chocolate and roast malt flavor. The published 5.3% ABV sits at the top of that range.
