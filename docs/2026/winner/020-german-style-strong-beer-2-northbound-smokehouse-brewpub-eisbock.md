# 020-german-style-strong-beer-2-northbound-smokehouse-brewpub-eisbock

Eisbock is made each year by Northbound Smokehouse Brewpub in Minneapolis by fractionally freezing part of its 8.5% doppelbock in kegs and drawing off the concentrated beer.

## brewery-description

The brewery publishes no description of the beer itself; its founder's account of the process says the beer is frozen in a chest freezer at about 0°F (minus 18°C) until about half has frozen over 30 to 36 hours, with advice to "Shoot for over-freezing."

Source: [Craft Beer and Brewing: Eisbock It with Northbound](https://www.beerandbrewing.com/brewers-perspective-eisbock-it)

## ingredients

Founder Jamie Robinson's article names no grain bill, hop or yeast, saying only that "High-quality ingredients and clean fermentation are key" ([Craft Beer and Brewing](https://www.beerandbrewing.com/brewers-perspective-eisbock-it)). Brewer Andy Rosenthal's published recipe for the base doppelbock (original gravity 1.083, final gravity 1.019, 18 IBU, 8.5% ABV) sits behind a paywall, so its malts and yeast could not be read ([Craft Beer and Brewing recipe](https://www.beerandbrewing.com/recipe-northbound-doppelbock-and-eisbock)).

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for German-Style Eisbock describes a stronger doppelbock concentrated by freezing ("Traditionally, these beers were created by freezing a Doppelbock and removing the ice"), at 8.6 to 14.3 percent ABV and 26 to 33 IBU, light brown to black. The method matches the definition exactly; with no tested ABV or IBU, the numbers cannot be compared.

## research-notes

- The brewery's founder writes that the eisbock is targeted at 16 to 17 percent ABV and about 36 IBU but that neither has been tested; per the no estimate rule, both are recorded as not published.
- Earlier awards: GABF gold for this eisbock in 2024 and 2025 (Craft Beer and Brewing, December 2025), and a 2024 World Beer Cup gold for the doppelbock. The brewery's awards page lists the World Beer Cup gold but no GABF medals.
- The current brewpub menu does not list the eisbock; its only bock is Wild Rice Amber Bock, a different beer. There is no beer-url.
