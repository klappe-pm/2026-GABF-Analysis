## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Italian-Style Pilsener calls for assertive, crisp and aromatic hop character, 4.5 to 5.3 percent ABV, 25 to 50 IBU and 3 to 4 SRM. The published 4.8% ABV and 35 IBU fit.
