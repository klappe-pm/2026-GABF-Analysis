## brewery-description

> Our Dank and Juicy Hazy IPA brewed in collaboration with our buds at Flatland Brewing. Featuring all Southern Hemisphere hops from Freestyle Hop Farms. This hazy is packed with Nectaron, Peacharine and Motueka Hops bringing out flavors of passionfruit, peach rings, melon, lychee and Pure Dankness.

Source: [MachineHead: tap list](https://machineheadbrewing.com/tap-list/)

## ingredients

Both the [beers page](https://machineheadbrewing.com/beers/) and the [tap list](https://machineheadbrewing.com/tap-list/) name the same three hops, Nectaron, Peacharine and Motueka; the tap list adds that they come from Freestyle Hop Farms. No malt or yeast is named.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Juicy or Hazy Strong Pale Ale describes a stronger hazy ale with juicy, fruity hop flavor and soft, well-integrated bitterness, 5.6 to 6.4 percent ABV and 15 to 40 IBU. The published 6.8% ABV is above that range; the current listing may not match the entry.
