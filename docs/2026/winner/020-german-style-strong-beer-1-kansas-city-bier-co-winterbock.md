# 020-german-style-strong-beer-1-kansas-city-bier-co-winterbock

Winterbock is a dark, strong doppelbock lager from Kansas City Bier Co. in Kansas City, Missouri, brewed with German imported malt and hops and double decocted.

## brewery-description

> full-bodied, dark doppelbock-style lager with rich malt flavors of chocolate and caramel ... with hints of coffee, low bitterness, and a satisfying smooth finish.

Source: [Craft Beer and Brewing: Winterbock review](https://www.beerandbrewing.com/review/winterbock)

## ingredients

The brewery's 2021 release text, carried by Porch Drinking, says Winterbock uses "100% German-imported malt and hops" and that "Double decoction and natural carbonation provide a smooth, clean finish" ([Porch Drinking](https://www.porchdrinking.com/?p=204145)). No malt or hop variety and no yeast is named.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for German-Style Doppelbock calls for a strong, full-bodied lager, copper to dark brown, at 6.6 to 7.9 percent ABV and 17 to 27 IBU, where "Malty sweetness is pronounced but should not be cloying." The 24 IBU fits; the 8.25% ABV sits above the doppelbock range, which suits this broader strong beer category.
