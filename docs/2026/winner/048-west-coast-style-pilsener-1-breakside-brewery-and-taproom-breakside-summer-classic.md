# 048-west-coast-style-pilsener-1-breakside-brewery-and-taproom-breakside-summer-classic

Summer Classic is a fruity summer beer hopped with Thora and Krush that Breakside Brewery released with the Hillsboro Hops minor league baseball team; the brewery's page describes it as an IPA, so the pilsener entry that medaled may differ from the published recipe.

## ingredients

The [brewery page](https://breakside.com/our_beer/summer-classic/) names Thora hops (guava and passionfruit) and Krush hops (wild raspberry, melon and rhubarb) on a Pilsner and 2-row malt base. No yeast is named, and the page does not say whether the beer is fermented as an ale or a lager.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for West Coast-Style Pilsener describes an assertive, crisp lager with bold modern hop character and a body built to carry heavy dry hopping, at 5.0 to 6.0 percent ABV and 30 to 50 IBU. The published 6% ABV and 42 IBU fall inside those ranges, though the published specs describe the IPA.
