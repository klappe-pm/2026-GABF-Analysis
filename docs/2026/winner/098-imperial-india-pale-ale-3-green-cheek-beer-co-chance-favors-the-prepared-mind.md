## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for American-Style Imperial or Double IPA calls for straw to medium amber color, high to intense fresh American hop character, very high bitterness and medium-high to high alcohol, at 7.6 to 10.6 percent ABV and 65 to 100 IBU. The listed 8.6% ABV fits that range; no IBU is published.
