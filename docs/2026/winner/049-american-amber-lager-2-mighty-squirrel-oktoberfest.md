## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for American-Style Amber Lager calls for low to medium-low caramel or toasted malt, 4.8 to 5.4 percent ABV, 18 to 30 IBU and 6 to 14 SRM. The listed 5.5% ABV sits just above the top of that range; IBU cannot be checked.
