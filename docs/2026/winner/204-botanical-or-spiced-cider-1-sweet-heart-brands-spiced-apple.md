# 204-botanical-or-spiced-cider-1-sweet-heart-brands-spiced-apple

Spiced Apple is a cinnamon cider poured on tap at the Loveland and Fort Collins tasting rooms of Sweet Heart Brands, the Northern Colorado company behind Sweet Heart Winery and the Climb Hard, Patriot Press and Sweetheart cider brands.
