# 204-botanical-or-spiced-cider-1-sweet-heart-brands-spiced-apple

Spiced Apple is a cinnamon cider poured on tap at the Loveland and Fort Collins tasting rooms of Sweet Heart Brands, the Northern Colorado company behind Sweet Heart Winery and the Climb Hard, Patriot Press and Sweetheart cider brands.

## ingredients

The menu notes only that the cider contains cinnamon. The house cider on the same menu is described as one that "blends apple varieties for a crisp, refreshing cider," but the menu does not say whether Spiced Apple shares that base ([Sweet Heart Winery: menu](https://www.sweetheartwinery.com/menu)).

## style-context

As category context only, the [GABF 2026 style categories](https://www.greatamericanbeerfestival.com/the-competition/about-the-competition/beer-styles) say Botanical or Spiced Cider "focuses on ciders with added botanicals (including herbs, spices, hops, vegetables, chilis)," at 3 to 10 percent ABV. The Brewers Association beer style guidelines carry no cider entries. Cinnamon is a spice addition; no ABV is published to compare.
