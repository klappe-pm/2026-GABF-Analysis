## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for West Coast-Style India Pale Ale calls for straw to gold color, low to medium-low malt with no caramel or roast, high to very high assertive hop character, medium to very high bitterness, high attenuation and a dry, crisp finish, at 6.3 to 7.5 percent ABV and 50 to 75 IBU. The listed ABV fits this category range.
