# 018-session-india-pale-ale-2-offset-bier-co-dopo

Dopo is the house beer of Offset Bier Co. in Park City, Utah: a 5% hazy "L IPA" hopped with New Zealand and Pacific Northwest varieties, its name Italian for "after".

## brewery-description

> What's a L IPA you ask? We like to call these 5% IPAs we brew LiePAs, cause a 5% IPA is a lie. ... A blend of our favorite hops from the Pacific Northwest and Southern Hemisphere brings out modern fruit forward flavors

Source: [Offset Bier: tap list](https://offsetbier.com/taplist-1)

## ingredients

The tap list names Riwaka, Mosaic (as T90 pellets and as Lupocore) and Nelson, and says the hop load is closer to that of a 7% beer. In its Craft Beer and Brewing listing the brewery describes the beer as "designed to punch well above it's weight featuring our favorite hops from New Zealand and the Pacific Northwest" ([Craft Beer and Brewing](https://www.beerandbrewing.com/review/offset-bier-%2Ddopo-1710424461)). No malt or yeast is named.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Session India Pale Ale describes lower alcohol versions of any IPA style at 0.5 to 5.0 percent ABV and 20 to 55 IBU, with assertive hop character balanced for drinkability. The published 5% ABV and 54 IBU both sit at the top edge of the ranges.
