## ingredients

The brewery names Mittelfrüh as the hop and says "Noble Hops provide floral notes and a crisp finish" ([Logboat: Bobber](https://www.logboatbrewing.com/bobber)). No malt or yeast is named there or on [BeerMenus](https://www.beermenus.com/beers/213333-logboat-bobber).

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Czech-Style Pale Lager calls for a slightly sweet, toasted, biscuity or bready malt character, 4.6 to 5.1 percent ABV, 22 to 40 IBU and 4 to 7 SRM. The listed 5.0% ABV fits; IBU cannot be checked.
