## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Contemporary American-Style Lager describes a clean, crisp lager with more hop aroma and flavor than traditional American lager, at 4.1 to 5.1 percent ABV and 5 to 16 IBU. The listed 4.5% ABV fits; the listed 17 IBU is one unit above the range.
