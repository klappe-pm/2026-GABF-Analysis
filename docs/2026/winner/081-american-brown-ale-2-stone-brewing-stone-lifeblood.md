## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for American-Style Brown Ale calls for roasted, caramel and chocolate malt with balanced hop flavor, 4.2 to 6.3 percent ABV and 30 to 45 IBU. The published 6.8% ABV and 60 IBU both sit above those ranges.
