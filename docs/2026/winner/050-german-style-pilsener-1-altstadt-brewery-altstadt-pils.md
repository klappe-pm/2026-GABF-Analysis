## ingredients

The beer page names 100% German Pilsner malt with Hallertau Mittelfrüh and Saaz hops ([Altstadt: German Pils](https://altstadtbeer.com/beers/german-pils/)), and the brewery's medal post credits those hops with "floral aromas, light spice and firm but approachable bitterness" ([Altstadt: 2026 GABF news](https://altstadtbeer.com/in-the-news/altstadt-wins-two-gold-medals-2026-gabf/)). A visitor listing says the brewery uses only hops, barley, yeast and artesian spring water ([Visit Fredericksburg: Altstadt Brewery](https://visitfredericksburgtx.com/listing/altstadt-brewery/1113/)). No yeast strain is named.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for German-Style Pilsener calls for assertive, crisp hop character, 4.6 to 5.3 percent ABV, 25 to 50 IBU and 3 to 4 SRM. The published 5.1% ABV, 25 IBU and SRM 3 all fit, with bitterness at the low edge.
