## ingredients

The brewery names only sparkling water and Citra and Mosaic hops. A December 2023 trade report adds that the blend gives tropical fruit and citrus aromatics and that the water is carbonated with CO2 recaptured from fermentation ([The Brewer Magazine: release](https://thebrewermagazine.com/alvarado-street-releases-2-non-alcoholic-beverages/)).
