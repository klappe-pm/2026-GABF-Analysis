Source: [Alvarado Street: Froth Water](https://asb.beer/products/froth-water)

## ingredients

The brewery names only sparkling water and Citra and Mosaic hops. A December 2023 trade report adds that the blend gives tropical fruit and citrus aromatics and that the water is carbonated with CO2 recaptured from fermentation ([The Brewer Magazine: release](https://thebrewermagazine.com/alvarado-street-releases-2-non-alcoholic-beverages/)).

## style-context

As category context only, the [GABF 2026 style categories](https://www.greatamericanbeerfestival.com/the-competition/about-the-competition/beer-styles) define Hop Water as "a non-alcohol or alcohol-free sparkling beverage that showcases hop-derived aroma and flavor," with alcohol absent and no ABV range. The Brewers Association beer style guidelines carry no hop water entry. The published 0.0% hopped sparkling water fits the category.
