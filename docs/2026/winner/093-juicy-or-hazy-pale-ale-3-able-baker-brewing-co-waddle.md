## brewery-description

Light, crisp, and delicately refreshing this brew allows you to "Waddle" away and forge your own footprint.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Juicy or Hazy Pale Ale calls for a hazy beer with very prominent fruity, juicy hop character and soft bitterness, 4.4 to 5.4 percent ABV and 5 to 30 IBU. The 4.4% ABV sits at the bottom of the range and the 27 IBU fits.
