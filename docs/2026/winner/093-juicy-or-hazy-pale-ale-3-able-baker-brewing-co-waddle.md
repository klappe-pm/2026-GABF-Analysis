## brewery-description

> Well behaved ducks rarely make history, so we continue to break all the rules regarding style parameters. Here we craft a session ale on a light base of wheat and 2-row malts. The simplified malt bill and low ABV allow the beauty of Mosaic hops and fresh citrus to shine through. Light, crisp, and delicately refreshing this brew allows you to "Waddle" away and forge your own footprint.

Source: [Able Baker: downtown tap list](https://www.ablebakerbrewing.com/current-beer)

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Juicy or Hazy Pale Ale calls for a hazy beer with very prominent fruity, juicy hop character and soft bitterness, 4.4 to 5.4 percent ABV and 5 to 30 IBU. The 4.4% ABV sits at the bottom of the range and the 27 IBU fits.
