## ingredients

The [Tavour listing](https://beerdb.tavour.com/b/oni-giri-b490663) names Callista and Hallertau Blanc hops for floral notes and flaked rice in the mash for a melon note in the finish. No base malt or yeast is named.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Rice Lager describes a pale, clean lager in which rice adds subtle to expressive character, at 4.0 to 6.0 percent ABV and 15 to 30 IBU. The 4.5% ABV fits; no IBU is published to compare.
