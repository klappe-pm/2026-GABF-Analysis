# 017-session-beer-1-geisthaus-brewing-co-soulhound

Soulhound is a low alcohol Czech pale lager brewed entirely from floor malted Haná heritage barley by Geisthaus Brewing Co., an all lager brewery in Sacramento, California.

## brewery-description

> An ultra sessionable Czech Pale Lager. Mashed with 100% @admiralmaltings Haná malt, a heritage barley whose storied history involves the creation of the first Pilsner beers in the mid-1800s. Hopped with generous amounts of Czech Saaz in the kettle and whirlpool, fermented cool with our house lager yeast, spunded for natural carbonation, and lagered cold for weeks in horizontal tanks until prime crispiness was achieved. If you are in need of your next crusher that does not sacrifice on flavor, look no further.

Source: [Geisthaus: home page beer list](https://www.geisthausbeer.com/)

## ingredients

The grist is a single malt, Haná heritage barley floor malted by Admiral Maltings. Czech Saaz is the only hop, added in the kettle and whirlpool, and fermentation uses the brewery's unnamed house lager yeast.
