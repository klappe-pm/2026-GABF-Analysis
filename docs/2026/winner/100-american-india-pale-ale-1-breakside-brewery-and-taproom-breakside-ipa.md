# 100-american-india-pale-ale-1-breakside-brewery-and-taproom-breakside-ipa

Breakside IPA is the year-round flagship IPA and top selling beer of Breakside Brewery in Oregon, a clear, light copper take on the Northwest IPA.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for American-Style India Pale Ale calls for pale to copper color, medium-low to medium malt, high to very high American hop character and medium-high to very high bitterness, at 6.3 to 7.5 percent ABV and 50 to 70 IBU. The published 6.8% ABV and 62 IBU fit inside both ranges.
