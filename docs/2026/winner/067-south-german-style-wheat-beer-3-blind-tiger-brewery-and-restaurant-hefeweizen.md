## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for South German-Style Hefeweizen call for a highly carbonated wheat ale with banana and clove character and minimal hops, at 4.9 to 5.6 percent ABV and 10 to 15 IBU. The listed 4.8% ABV and 17 IBU each sit just outside those ranges.
