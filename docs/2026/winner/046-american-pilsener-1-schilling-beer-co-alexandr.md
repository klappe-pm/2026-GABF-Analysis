## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for American-Style Pilsener describes a pre-Prohibition style lager with medium-high noble-type hop character over medium-low to medium malt, at 4.9 to 6.0 percent ABV and 25 to 40 IBU. The 5% ABV fits; the selected 25 IBU also fits.
