As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Specialty Honey Beer says these beers "incorporate honey as a fermentable sugar in addition to malted barley," with honey character "present in aroma and flavor but should not be overpowering," at 2 to 9.5 percent ABV and 1 to 100 IBU. The listed strength and bitterness fall within those category ranges.
