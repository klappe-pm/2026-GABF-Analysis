# 045-international-style-pilsener-3-breakside-brewery-and-taproom-breakside-the-sublime

The Sublime is Breakside Brewery's helles lager, first brewed with Templin Family Brewing of Salt Lake City, that keeps a traditional helles malt and bitterness profile but adds extra whirlpool hop aroma.

## brewery-description

The brewery page frames lagers as "refreshing, smooth, drinkable, clean, and complex" and explains that hops go into the whirlpool at a "Pilsner level" after cooling, which adds herbal and floral aroma without much bitterness.

Source: [Breakside: The Sublime](https://breakside.com/our_beer/the-sublime/)

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for International-Style Pilsener describes a low to medium malt and hop lager that may show corn, rice or wheat adjunct character, at 4.6 to 5.3 percent ABV and 15 to 40 IBU. The 5.2% ABV and 18 IBU both fit.
