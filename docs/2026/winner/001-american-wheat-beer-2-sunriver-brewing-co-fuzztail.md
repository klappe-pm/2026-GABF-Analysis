## brewery-description

> Our gold medal-winning American style hefeweizen is the perfect partner for after work or after play. Idaho-grown pale and wheat malts create a gentle sweetness with bready notes. A blend of hand-selected PNW hops gives this hazy, refreshing wheat beer a crisp finish with pleasant notes of grapefruit, lemon, and evergreen.
