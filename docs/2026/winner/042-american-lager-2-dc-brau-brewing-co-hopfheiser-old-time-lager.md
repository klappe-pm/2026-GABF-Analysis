# 042-american-lager-2-dc-brau-brewing-co-hopfheiser-old-time-lager

Old Time Lager is a light bodied American lager of 2-row barley and flaked corn, sold under the revived Hopfheiser Brewing Company name and brewed in Washington by DC Brau Brewing Co.

## ingredients

The 2022 launch release, quoted by [DC Beer](https://dcbeer.com/2022/04/05/old-time-lager-relaunches-in-dc/) and repeated on [Untappd](https://untappd.com/b/the-hopfheiser-brewing-company-old-time-lager/4784176), says the beer is brewed with North American 2-row barley and flaked corn for a crisp, dry finish and light body. No hops or yeast are named.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for American-Style Lager describes a very clean, crisp, highly carbonated lager often made with corn or rice, at 4.1 to 5.1 percent ABV and 5 to 15 IBU. The listed 4% ABV is a touch under the range; no IBU is published to compare.

## research-notes

- Field pass 2026-10-13: Searches found no new IBU or hop varieties for DC Brau's Hopfheiser Old Time Lager.
- The brand site credits The Hopfheiser Brewing Company and does not name DC Brau; DC Beer reports that DC Brau bought the Hopfheiser and Old Time names and brews the beer, and Untappd lists it as brewed by DC Brau.
- The beer revives a 1935 Hopfheiser light lager originally brewed at Baltimore's Globe Brewing Company.
- The brand site lists no ABV or IBU; the 4% figure comes from Untappd and DC Beer.
- The DC Brau awards page lists a 2023 US Beer Open bronze for Old Time Lager and nothing for 2026.
