## ingredients

The cidery states "Made with 100% Harrison apples," a variety it says was nearly lost to extinction and revived in 1976, with flavor notes of juicy apple, orange peel, earthy spice, lemon and ginger ([Snow Capped: Harrison](https://snowcappedcider.com/all-ciders/harrison/)).

## style-context

As category context only, the [GABF 2026 style categories](https://www.greatamericanbeerfestival.com/the-competition/about-the-competition/beer-styles) say No/Low Tannin Cider "focuses on modern interpretations of cider often utilizing apples or pears of culinary interest," at 3 to 10 percent ABV. The Brewers Association beer style guidelines carry no cider entries. The published 6.5% fits the range, and the brewery describes only soft tannin.
