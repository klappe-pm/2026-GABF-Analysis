# 201-tannic-cider-2-gowans-heirloom-cider-1876

1876 is a medium-dry, lightly sparkling cider pressed from estate-grown organic heirloom apples by Gowan's Heirloom Cider in Philo, California, named for the year the family planted in Anderson Valley.
