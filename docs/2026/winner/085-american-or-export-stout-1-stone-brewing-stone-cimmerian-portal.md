## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) describe American-Style Stout as black and roasty with medium to high American hop character, 5.7 to 8.0 percent ABV and 35 to 60 IBU, and Export-Style Stout as a strong, dry roasted stout at 5.6 to 8.0 percent ABV and 30 to 60 IBU. The published 6.5% ABV fits both.
