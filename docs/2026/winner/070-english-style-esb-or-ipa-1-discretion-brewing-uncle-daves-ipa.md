## brewery-description

> Is there anyone better to sit around a campfire with, telling stories, IPAs in hand? We think not. Cheers, Uncle Dave! Our Rye IPA has fruity/herbal hop aromas with a substantial supporting maltiness. The rye contributes a hint of peppery spice to this full bodied, complex IPA.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) cover the two styles in this category: Extra Special Bitter (4.8 to 5.8 percent ABV, 30 to 45 IBU) and British-Style India Pale Ale (4.5 to 7.1 percent ABV, 35 to 63 IBU, assertive English hop character balanced by malt). The published 6.5% ABV fits the British IPA range, while 68 IBU sits above it.
