Spruce tips are the only ingredient the brewery names. Untappd describes the beer as an "Imperial Red on Spruce Tips" and files it as an imperial red ale ([Untappd: Spruce Goose](https://untappd.com/b/babybat-brewery-spruce-goose/5214225)); no hop, malt or yeast names are published.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Herb and Spice Beer asks for herb or spice character from subtle to intense, favors very low to medium-low bitterness so that character shows, and lets IBU and ABV vary with the base style. Spruce tips are a classic herbal addition, and the 9% strong ale base is allowed under the varying ABV.
