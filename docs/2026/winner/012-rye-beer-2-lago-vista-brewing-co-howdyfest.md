As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Rye Beer says "The grist should include sufficient rye so that rye character is evident in the beer," accepts a wide range of color, and lets ABV and IBU vary with the underlying style. The brewery's note of rye spice on a toasty lager base matches that requirement.
