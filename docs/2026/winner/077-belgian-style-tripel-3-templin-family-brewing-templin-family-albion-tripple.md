## brewery-description

> Back in 1998 The Templin's hiked up Albion Basin and said, "I do" at 10,200 ft elevation. Belgian-Style Trippel brewed at 10.2% (wink). Brewed with classic Trappist yeast. Deceivingly alcoholic, light body, effervescent.

Source: [Templin Family: Albion Trippel](https://tfbrewing.com/beer/albion-trippel/)

## ingredients

The brewery names Czech Saaz and German Tettnang hops and a classic Trappist yeast without naming the strain ([Templin Family: Albion Trippel](https://tfbrewing.com/beer/albion-trippel/)). No malt or adjunct is listed.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Belgian-Style Tripel calls for 7.1 to 10.1 percent ABV, 20 to 50 IBU and 4 to 9 SRM, with a complex, sometimes mildly spicy aroma and flavor. The published 10.2% ABV sits just above the guideline's upper limit; no IBU is published.
