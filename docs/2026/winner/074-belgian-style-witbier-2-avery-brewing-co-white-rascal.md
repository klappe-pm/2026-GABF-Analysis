## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Belgian-Style Witbier calls for 4.0 to 5.5 percent ABV, 10 to 17 IBU and 2 to 4 SRM, traditionally bottle conditioned and served cloudy. The published 5.6% ABV and the third party 20 IBU each sit slightly above the guideline ranges.
