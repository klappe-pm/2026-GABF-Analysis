## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for International-Style Pilsener describes a low to medium malt and hop lager that may show corn, rice or wheat adjunct character, at 4.6 to 5.3 percent ABV and 15 to 40 IBU. The 4.7% ABV and 19 IBU both fit.
