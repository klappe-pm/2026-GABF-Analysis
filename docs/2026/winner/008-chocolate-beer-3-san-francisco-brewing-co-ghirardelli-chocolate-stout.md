## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Chocolate or Cocoa Beer asks that chocolate or cocoa character be apparent and blend with the base beer, with malt sweetness in balance, very low to medium-low bitterness, and IBU and ABV varying with the base style. A stout with chocolate malt and cacao nibs fits squarely.

## research-notes

- Both location menus list the beer as Ghirardelli Chocolate Imperial Stout at 9.5%; the winners list name omits "Imperial". It is treated as the same beer, which is an inference from the matching name.
- A BeerAdvocate entry seen only as a search snippet (not opened) lists a Ghirardelli Chocolate Nitro Stout at 8%; it may be an earlier nitro version and is not used.
- IBU, hops and yeast are not published.
- Researched 2026-10-13.
- Field pass 2026-10-13: The plain Chocolate Stout listing has N/A IBU and no hops. Its 5% ABV differs from the existing brewery Imperial Stout value of 9.5%, which is retained. Chocolate-Coffee variants advertise 50 IBU in search results and were excluded.
