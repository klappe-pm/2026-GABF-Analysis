## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Juicy or Hazy India Pale Ale calls for straw to light amber color with variable haze, low to medium-low malt, medium-high to very high juicy or tropical hop character and soft bitterness, at 6.3 to 7.5 percent ABV and 20 to 50 IBU. The listed ABV and IBU fit these category ranges.
