## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Scottish-Style Export Ale call for a medium-bodied amber to chestnut ale led by sweet malt and caramel with no hop character, at 4.1 to 6.0 percent ABV and 15 to 25 IBU. The published 5.5% ABV fits; 12 IBU is below that range.
