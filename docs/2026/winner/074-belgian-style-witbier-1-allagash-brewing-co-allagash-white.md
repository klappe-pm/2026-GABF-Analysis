## ingredients

The brewery publishes a full grain bill of a 2-row barley blend, local pale malt, red wheat malt, raw white wheat, oats and Carapils, hopped with Nugget, Crystal and Czech Saaz, fermented with its house yeast and spiced with its own coriander and Curaçao orange peel blend ([Allagash: White](https://www.allagash.com/beer/white/)).

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Belgian-Style Witbier calls for 4.0 to 5.5 percent ABV, 10 to 17 IBU and 2 to 4 SRM, traditionally bottle conditioned and served cloudy. The published 5.2% ABV and the third party 13 IBU both fit.
