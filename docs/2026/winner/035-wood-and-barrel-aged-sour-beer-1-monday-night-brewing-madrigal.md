## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Wood- and Barrel-Aged Sour Beer covers lager, ale or hybrid beers aged in wood or in contact with wood, with acidity from bacteria; ABV, IBU and color vary with the underlying style. A barrel-aged oud bruin fits that description.
