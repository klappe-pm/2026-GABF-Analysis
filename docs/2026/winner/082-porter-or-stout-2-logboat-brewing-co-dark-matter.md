## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) read for this profile list porters and stouts as separate styles; Robust Porter calls for a very dark beer with cocoa and caramel sweetness balanced by dark malt bitterness, 5.1 to 6.6 percent ABV and 25 to 40 IBU. The published 6.6% ABV and 25 IBU sit at the edges of that range.
