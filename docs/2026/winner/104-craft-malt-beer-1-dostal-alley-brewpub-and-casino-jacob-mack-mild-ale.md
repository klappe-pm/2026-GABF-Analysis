## style-context

As category context only, the Brewers Association's [craft malt category announcement](https://www.brewersassociation.org/association-news/gabf-debuts-a-craft-malt-category-in-2026/) requires at least 50% of the grain bill from a craft maltster (under 10,000 metric tons a year, more than half its grain grown within 500 miles) and lets color, aroma, flavor and body deviate from a stated base style to highlight the malt. Root Shoot Malting is named as the maltster, but its qualifying status and the share of the grain bill are not published.
