## brewery-description

> Counter Weight Grodziskie is modeled after traditional small wheat Polish smoked beers. Brewed with 100% oak-smoked wheat and a hearty dose of Czech-sourced hops. Mild but inviting smokey notes in its aroma, with soft smoke and herbal hops meld and bring you back again and again for more. A small beer at just 3.4% ABV that brings a ton of flavor and depth.

Source: [Counter Weight: all brews](https://www.counterweightbrewing.com/brews/)

## ingredients

The brewery names a single grain, oak-smoked wheat, making up the entire grist, and hops sourced from the Czech Republic without naming the varieties ([Counter Weight: all brews](https://www.counterweightbrewing.com/brews/)). No yeast strain is published.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) define Historical Beer as established historical beers or brewing traditions from any era, with parameters following the underlying style; the separate Grodziskie guideline calls for a grain bill entirely of oak-smoked wheat malt, 2.5 to 3.3 percent ABV and 20 to 30 IBU. The published all oak-smoked wheat grist matches the style, while the 3.4% ABV sits just above the Grodziskie range.
