## brewery-description

> Counter Weight Grodziskie is modeled after traditional small wheat Polish smoked beers. Brewed with 100% oak-smoked wheat and a hearty dose of Czech-sourced hops. Mild but inviting smokey notes in its aroma, with soft smoke and herbal hops meld and bring you back again and again for more. A small beer at just 3.4% ABV that brings a ton of flavor and depth.
