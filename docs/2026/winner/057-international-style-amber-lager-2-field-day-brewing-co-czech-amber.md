## brewery-description

> A unique blend of toasty and bready malt characteristics with a refreshing kick of Czech Saaz hops. This beer was double decocted to enhance the malt richness you would expect from an amber and then hopped assertively with Saaz. The perfect Yin and Yang.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) page lists no style named International-Style Amber Lager; it does list Czech-Style Amber Lager, and its nearby Vienna-Style Lager entry calls for 4.8 to 5.4 percent ABV and 20 to 26 IBU with light toasted or caramel malt. The published 4.7% ABV sits just under that Vienna range.
