## brewery-description

> The beer that came out of that thought experiment is filled with three of the fruitiest hops around, Strata, Mosaic, and Azacca, along with a touch of Simcoe to anchor the beer in classic dank and catty West Coast flavor. The bitterness is modest.

Source: [Breakside: Wanderjack](https://breakside.com/our_beer/wanderjack/)

## ingredients

The [brewery page](https://breakside.com/our_beer/wanderjack/) spec list gives Strata, Mosaic, Simcoe and Amarillo hops on a lean 2-row and Vienna malt bill; the description names Azacca instead of Amarillo, so all five are listed. No yeast is named, and the page does not say whether the beer is fermented as an ale or a lager.

## style-context

The [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) page read for this run lists no style named Hoppy Lager. As loose category context only, its nearest hop forward lager style, American-Style India Pale Lager, calls for high hop aroma and flavor at 5.6 to 7.9 percent ABV and 50 to 70 IBU; the published 7.1% ABV and 63 IBU fall inside those ranges, though the published specs describe the IPA.
