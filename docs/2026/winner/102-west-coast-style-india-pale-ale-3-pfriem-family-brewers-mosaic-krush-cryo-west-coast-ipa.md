## brewery-description

> This West Coast IPA is a meticulous exploration of clarity, snap, and modern hop saturations.

Source: [pFriem: Mosaic + Krush Cryo West Coast IPA](https://www.pfriembeer.com/beer/mosaic-krush-cryo-west-coast-ipa/)

The page adds that it is crystalline in appearance and aggressively aromatic, with a clean, drying finish.

## tasting-notes

> This West Coast IPA pours a golden straw and is crowned with a thick cap of rocky white foam. The aroma opens with pungent tropical fruits like pineapple and mango followed by notes of juicy peach and ripe kiwi. The palate brings layers of mixed berry, sweet citrus, white wine and candy. The beer finishes very tropical with assertive bitterness and saturation.

Source: [pfriembeer.com](https://www.pfriembeer.com/beer/mosaic-krush-cryo-west-coast-ipa/)

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for West Coast-Style India Pale Ale calls for straw to gold color, low to medium-low malt with no caramel or roast, high to very high assertive hop character, medium to very high bitterness, high attenuation and a dry, crisp finish, at 6.3 to 7.5 percent ABV and 50 to 75 IBU. The published 7.2% ABV and 55 IBU fit inside both ranges.
