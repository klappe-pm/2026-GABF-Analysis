## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Mexican-Style Light Lager calls for a clean fermentation profile, a very light to light body with a dry finish, 3.2 to 4.2 percent ABV, 8 to 17 IBU and a straw to pale gold color (SRM 2 to 3). The published 3.9% ABV falls inside the range.
