## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for American-Style Sour Ale call for medium-low to high natural acidity from bacteria and yeasts, fruity esters, no Brettanomyces, and ABV and IBU that may vary widely; that guideline places fruited versions elsewhere. The low ABV, lactobacillus souring and absence of any named Brettanomyces fit the sour profile, while the guava addition is a fruited element the guideline would usually route to another category.
