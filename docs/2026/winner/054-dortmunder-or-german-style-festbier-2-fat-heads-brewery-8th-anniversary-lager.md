## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) covers two styles in this category: Dortmunder/European-Style Export (5.1 to 6.1 percent ABV, 23 to 30 IBU, low bready malt that should not be caramelly) and German-Style Oktoberfest/Festbier (5.1 to 6.1 percent ABV, 23 to 29 IBU, clean, sweet, bready malt). The published strength fits either category range; its bitterness is slightly above both. Untappd labels the beer Dortmunder / Export.
