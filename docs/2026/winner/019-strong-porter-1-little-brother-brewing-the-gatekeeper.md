## ingredients

The brewery's tasting notes credit caramel and toffee notes to crystal malt and name roasted malt and roasted barley, with coffee, dark chocolate and a hint of black licorice on the palate. Hops appear only as "a subtle earthy hop presence" and a balanced bitterness, with no variety named. No yeast or IBU is given.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) closest to this competition category are Robust Porter (5.1 to 6.6 percent ABV, 25 to 40 IBU, very dark brown to black and roasty) and Baltic-Style Porter (7.6 to 9.3 percent ABV, lager fermented). The published 6% ABV and near black color fit Robust Porter.
