## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Gluten-Free Beer require every ingredient to be free of gluten and let ABV, IBU and color vary with the underlying style, judging entries on their own merits; the Pumpkin Spice Beer guideline it resembles calls for pumpkin or squash as an adjunct with spices such as cinnamon, allspice, clove and nutmeg in balance.
