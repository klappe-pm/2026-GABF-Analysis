## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for International Light Lager calls for no malt sweetness, often sugar adjuncts to lighten body and flavor, 4.1 to 5.1 percent ABV, 9 to 18 IBU and a straw to gold color (SRM 2 to 5). The 18 IBU sits at the top of the range, while the 3% ABV listed on Untappd is below the guideline range.
