Local press reports that both of the brewery's 2026 golds "were for coffee beers brewed with java from Bird Rock Coffee Roasters" ([San Diego Beer News](https://sandiegobeer.news/gabf-2026-winners/)). Whether Beatnik is a stout or a porter, and what malts it uses, is not published.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) Coffee Beer entry states that "Coffee Stouts or Porters should be entered in the Coffee Stout or Porter category," and describes coffee beers generally as beers that "incorporate coffee in any form." The dedicated Coffee Stout or Porter entry was not in the portion of the guideline page that could be read, so no ranges are given here.
