## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Irish-Style Red Ale call for a copper-red ale with caramel sweetness, toasted malt and a slight roast finish, at 4.0 to 4.8 percent ABV and 20 to 28 IBU. The menu's 5.5% ABV and 32 IBU are both above those ranges, and the menu labels it an American amber rather than an Irish red.
