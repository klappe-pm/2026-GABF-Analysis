## ingredients

Raspberry puree and a German hefeweizen base are the only ingredients named; the brewery's Untappd entry says "a ton of raspberry puree" went in ([Untappd: Raspberry Rush](https://untappd.com/b/armored-cow-brewing-co-raspberry-rush/5728670)). No hop, malt or yeast names are published.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Fruit Wheat Beer asks for fruit that expresses true fruit complexity, often more muted to blend with the wheat malt, at 10 to 35 IBU and 2.5 to 12 percent ABV. The published 5.4% ABV fits; IBU is not published.
