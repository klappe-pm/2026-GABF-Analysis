## brewery-description

> Our hop forward session beer, built for enjoying any time of day. You'll find this brew to be full of juicy, classic hop flavor with just enough malt backbone to keep things in balance. We reserve some of our favorite hops, Simcoe, Cascade and Centennial, to use in this beer, which gives the aroma a heady mix of orange marmalade, grapefruit, and resin.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for International-Style Pale Ale calls for an easy-drinking, well-integrated ale with varied hop character, 4.4 to 6.6 percent ABV and 20 to 42 IBU. Every published ABV (4.7%, 5.6%, 5.7%) and the 31 IBU fall inside those ranges.
