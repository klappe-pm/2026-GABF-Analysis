## brewery-description

> Designed to be supremely crushable, this easy drinking pilsner is layered with a nuanced complexity that keeps you wanting more. Brewed with our favorite German pilsner malt and complemented by a generous helping of Adeena hops from the good folks at Roy Farms. Crunchy Boi is dry and lightly bready with an undercurrent of lightly fruity and floral notes.

Source: [Ladd & Lass: the beer](https://www.laddandlassbrewing.com/the-beer)

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for American-Style Pilsener describes a pre-Prohibition style lager with medium-high noble-type hop character over medium-low to medium malt, at 4.9 to 6.0 percent ABV and 25 to 40 IBU. The 5.3% ABV fits, and the rice in the grist echoes the adjunct tradition of the style; no IBU is published.
