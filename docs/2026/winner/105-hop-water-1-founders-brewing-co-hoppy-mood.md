# 105-hop-water-1-founders-brewing-co-hoppy-mood

Hoppy Mood is a year-round, zero alcohol sparkling hop water from Founders Brewing Co. of Grand Rapids, Michigan, steeped with three hop varieties and finished with botanical terpenes.

## ingredients

The brewery's explainer says the drink is "simply made up of carbonated water, hops, and natural terpenes." De-oxygenated brewing water is pH adjusted with citric acid, Mosaic, Amarillo and Simcoe steep in the tank for several days and are then removed, and natural terpenes are added for grapefruit and pine character ([Founders: hop water explainer](https://foundersbrewing.com/latest-news/2024/exploring-hoppy-mood-a-delicious-and-refreshing-hop-water/)).
