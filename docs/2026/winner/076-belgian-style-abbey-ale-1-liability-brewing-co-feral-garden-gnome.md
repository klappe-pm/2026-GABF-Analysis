## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) has no style named Belgian-Style Abbey Ale; its nearest abbey style, Belgian-Style Dubbel, calls for 6.3 to 7.6 percent ABV and 20 to 40 IBU. The published 6.7% ABV falls inside that range, though the brewery labels the beer a blonde rather than a dubbel.
