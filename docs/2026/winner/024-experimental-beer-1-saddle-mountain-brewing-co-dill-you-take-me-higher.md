## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Experimental Beer covers beers that "employ unique and unusual techniques and/or ingredients", with ABV, IBU and color varying widely. A pickle infused sour fits that open definition.
