# 030-brett-beer-1-wishful-thinking-brewing-co-on-any-given-day

## ingredients

The brewery's drink menu lists the malts as Double Eagle (PA) Pilsner and wheat and the hops as NY Cascade, and the style line says the beer is dry-hopped and barrel aged ([Wishful Thinking: drink menu PDF](https://www.wishfulthinkingbeer.com/_files/ugd/b0b5c3_d4cffe93adbe40adba4c62defa641039.pdf)). The menu notes "light funk" but names no yeast or Brettanomyces strain.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Brett Beer call for Brettanomyces character at low to high levels, medium to high fruity esters, low to medium-low acidity, no bacteria, and ABV and IBU that vary with the underlying style; wood vessels may be used. A 6.1% barrel aged saison described with light funk and citrus fits that frame, though the menu does not itself name Brettanomyces.
