## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) describes hoppier non-alcohol beers "ranging from Pale Ales to IPA" that "should showcase a prominent hop presence emblematic of hop-forward pale ale to India Pale Ale profiles," under the non-alcohol cap of less than 0.5% ABV.
