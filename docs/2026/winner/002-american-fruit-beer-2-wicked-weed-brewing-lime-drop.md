## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guideline](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for American-Style Fruit Beer calls for fruit aroma from subtle to intense that is not overpowered by hops, bitterness from 5 to 70 IBU in balance with the fruit, and 2.5 to 12 percent ABV. Lime Drop's published 4.8% ABV fits; its IBU is not published.
