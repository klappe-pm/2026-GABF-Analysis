## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Gluten-Free Beer require every ingredient to be free of gluten, exclude barley, wheat, spelt and rye, and let ABV, IBU and color vary with the underlying style, judging entries on their own merits. The same guidelines describe Altbier as a copper to dark brown Dusseldorf ale with medium to high bitterness, 4.6 to 5.6 percent ABV and 25 to 52 IBU.
