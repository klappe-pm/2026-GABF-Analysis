## ingredients

No recipe for the Altbier is published. The brewery says all of its beers are brewed on site with naturally gluten-free malt made from local ancient grains "like millet and buckwheat", sourced from its sister company Grouse Malt House ([The Well: FAQ](https://www.wellwellington.com/faq)); that is a brewery-wide statement, so no grain is entered for this beer.

## style-context

As category context only, the [Brewers Association guidelines](https://www.brewersassociation.org/edu/brewers-association-beer-style-guidelines/) for Gluten-Free Beer require every ingredient to be free of gluten, exclude barley, wheat, spelt and rye, and let ABV, IBU and color vary with the underlying style, judging entries on their own merits. The same guidelines describe Altbier as a copper to dark brown Dusseldorf ale with medium to high bitterness, 4.6 to 5.6 percent ABV and 25 to 52 IBU. With no published specs, fit cannot be assessed.
