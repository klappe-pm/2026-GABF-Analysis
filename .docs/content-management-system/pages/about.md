# About

About: what the site is, who it is for, and how it stays independent. Linked from the footer.

## `header`

Where: top of the page.

- `title`: About GABF Analysis
- `lede`: An independent, public analysis of Great American Beer Festival results. It turns the official winners lists into one linked record of every medal and explains what the results show.

## `audience`

Where: the audience section.

- `title`: Who It Is for
- `body`: Brewers first: benchmark against the field, choose which categories to enter, and study the beers that won. Beer fans, press, and analysts use the same public data.

## `offer`

Where: the section on what the site offers.

- `title`: What Is Here
- `medals`: Every medal from {{first_year}} to {{last_year}}, linked across breweries, categories, and beers.
- `beers`: The {{profile_year}} winning beers with their published specs and the brewery's own description.
- `findings`: Written findings, each traceable to the data.

## `independence`

Where: the independence section.

- `title`: Independence
- `body`: GABF Analysis is unofficial. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Brewers Association or the Great American Beer Festival.

## `attribution`

Where: the attribution section.

- `title`: Attribution
- `trademark`: Great American Beer Festival and GABF are trademarks of the Brewers Association.
- `styles`: Style guideline content is quoted with attribution for noncommercial analysis.
- `breweries`: Brewery data from Open Brewery DB, an open database.
- `charts`: Charts drawn with Apache ECharts, used under the Apache 2.0 license.
- `maps`: US state boundaries from the us-atlas project, based on US Census Bureau cartographic boundaries.

## `links`

Where: the closing links.

- `method`: How the Data Is Built
