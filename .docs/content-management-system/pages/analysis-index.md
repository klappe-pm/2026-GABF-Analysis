# Analysis index

Analysis index: the list of written findings and ready made reports.

## `header`

Where: top of the page.

- `title`: Analysis
- `lede`: Findings from the medal record, each with the evidence behind it. Every report follows Filters, has a table for every chart, and saves charts as PNG.

## `findings`

Where: the findings list. Each finding's own copy lives in content/analysis.

- `title`: Findings
- `read_more`: Read the Finding

## `reports`

Where: the reports list, one line per report: the title, then the description.

- `title`: Reports

### `top_breweries`

- `title`: Top Breweries
- `description`: Medal and point leaders, and the longest active streaks.

### `top_categories`

- `title`: Top Categories
- `description`: The most entered categories, the hardest to medal in, and the style families that win the most.

### `by_state`

- `title`: By State
- `description`: Medals by state, per million residents, and per dollar of real GDP.

### `by_region`

- `title`: By Region
- `description`: Medals by Census region and division.

### `ownership`

- `title`: Ownership and Investors
- `description`: How many medals go to independent breweries, and who owns the rest.

### `economics`

- `title`: Economics
- `description`: Estimated barrels, ARR, margins, and distribution for every medalist.

### `trends`

- `title`: Trends
- `description`: Entries, medals, breweries, and style family share by year.

### `brewery_of_the_year`

- `title`: Brewery of the Year
- `description`: Brewery and Brewer of the Year awards by size bracket.

## `coverage`

Where: the year pills under the list.

- `pill`: {{year}} · {{coverage}}
