# Hop farms

The hop farms index: US hop farms grouped by state, with the varieties each publishes.

## `page`

Where: the top of the hop farms page and its browser tab.

- `title`: Hop Farms
- `lede`: US hop farms grouped by state, with the varieties each publishes. A farm reaches the medal beers in two separate ways: through the hops it grows, and by name when a brewery published the farm.
- `description`: US hop farms by state, the varieties each grows, and the medal beers linked to them.

## `links`

Where: the row of buttons under the lede.

- `hops`: All Hops

## `table`

Where: the column headers of each state table.

- `farm`: Farm
- `varieties`: Varieties
- `grown`: Medal Beers Using Hops Grown Here
- `named`: Medal Beers That Name This Farm
- `no_state`: State Not Found
- `sort_hint`: Select a column header to sort.

## `merchants`

Where: the hop merchants table under the states.

- `title`: Hop Merchants

## `gaps`

Where: the table under the merchants that states what the research could not find.

- `title`: What Is Not Known
- `note`: Each row names a question the sources checked could not answer, so a missing farm or variety is a gap and not a no.

## `states`

Where: in place of the tables when no hop farm is on record.

- `empty`: No hop farms are on record yet.
