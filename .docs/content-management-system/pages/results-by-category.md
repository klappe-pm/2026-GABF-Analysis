# Results by category

Results by category: the index of every category in the record.

## `header`

Where: top of the page.

- `title`: Results by Category
- `lede`: {{count}} categories in the record: {{special_count}} special and {{classic_count}} classic. Each shows its style family, the style's ABV range, and its latest entry count.

## `classic_and_special`

Where: the explainer card beside the chart.

- `title`: Classic and Special Categories
- `special`: A special category is judged on an added ingredient, a process, or a nonstandard base: fruit, spice, coffee, barrel aging, smoke, gluten-free, nonalcoholic, hybrid, specialty IPA, session beer, Pro-Am, collaboration, or cider. A classic category is judged against one style description.
- `families`: Style families group the official categories for browsing. They are this site's grouping, not an official one.
