# Hops

The hops index: every hop variety on record, with how many medal beers and medals use it.

## `page`

Where: the top of the hops page and its browser tab.

- `title`: Hops
- `lede`: Every hop variety named by a {{year}} medal beer or published for a US hop farm, with how many medal beers and medals use it. Open a variety for its data, its aroma, its acreage by state, and the farms that grow it.
- `note`: Counts cover the {{year}} medal beers whose hops are on record. A beer whose hops no source publishes is not counted. Select a column header to sort.
- `description`: Hop varieties used by {{year}} Great American Beer Festival medal beers, with medals, aroma, acreage, and the farms that grow each one.

## `links`

Where: the row of buttons under the lede.

- `farms`: Hop farms
- `winners`: {{year}} winners

## `stats`

Where: the tiles above the table.

- `varieties`: Hop varieties on record
- `used`: Varieties in medal beers
- `used_note`: In the {{year}} medal beers
- `farms`: Hop farms on record

## `table`

Where: the column headers of the hops table.

- `variety`: Hop variety
- `origin`: Origin
- `beers`: Medal beers
- `medals`: Medals
- `farms`: Farms that grow it
- `caption`: Hop varieties with their medal beers, medals, and the farms that grow them
- `sort_hint`: Select a column header to sort.

## `states`

Where: in place of the table when no hop variety is on record.

- `empty`: No hop varieties are on record yet.
