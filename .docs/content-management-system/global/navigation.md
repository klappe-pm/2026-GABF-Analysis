## `page_descriptions`

Where: meta description for search results and link previews.

- `home`: Independent analysis of Great American Beer Festival results: every medal, brewery, category, and winning beer, filterable on every page.
- `results_by_year`: Every medal from the {{year}} Great American Beer Festival.
- `results_by_category`: Every Great American Beer Festival category with entries, odds, and medalists.
- `category_detail`: {{category_name}}: style definition, entries by year, and every medalist.
- `breweries_index`: Every medal winning brewery in the record, ranked by points.
- `brewery_detail`: {{brewery_name}}: medal history, locations, ownership, distribution, and estimated scale.
- `design_system`: The tokens and components of GABF Analysis, shown with real medal data.
- `beers_index`: All {{count}} medal winning beers of {{year}} with published ABV, IBU, hops, malts, yeast, and adjuncts.
- `beer_detail`: {{beer_name}} by {{brewery_name}}: {{medal}} in {{category_name}}, {{year}}. Published specs and research notes.
- `first_time_medalists`: Breweries that won their first medal in the record in {{year}}.
- `analysis_index`: Written findings from the medal record: odds, concentration, geography, first time medalists, and style trends.
- `saved_views`: Views you saved on this device.
- `dashboards`: Interactive medal dashboards.
- `method`: Where the data comes from, what each year covers, and how estimates are made.
- `about`: What GABF Analysis is, who it is for, and how it stays independent.
- `not_found`: This page does not exist.
