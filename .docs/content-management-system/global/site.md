# Site

Site wide identity, header chrome, and footer.

## `identity`

Where: browser tab suffix, header brand, social cards, and the about page.

- `name`: GABF Analysis
- `tagline`: Every Great American Beer Festival medal, linked and explained.
- `description`: An independent analysis of Great American Beer Festival results: every medal from {{first_year}} to {{last_year}}, the breweries and beers that won them, and what the results show.

## `header`

Where: header, on every page.

- `skip_link`: Skip to content
- `menu_button`: Menu
- `nav_label`: Site

## `footer`

Where: footer, on every page.

- `sources`: Results come from the official Great American Beer Festival winners lists. Brewery locations come from Open Brewery DB with curated corrections.
- `estimates`: Figures marked (E) are estimates. See Method.
- `source_link`: Source and data
- `independence`: Independent and unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Brewers Association.
- `beer_details`: Some beer details are from Catalog.beer, licensed CC BY 4.0 and adapted.

## `single_file_footer`

Where: footer of the single file dashboard, after the sources line.

- `build_note`: Built from {{row_count}} medal rows across {{year_count}} years (latest: {{latest_year}}) and {{brewery_count}} breweries.

## `independence`

Where: about page attribution section and anywhere a trademark notice is required.

- `disclaimer`: GABF Analysis is an independent, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Brewers Association or the Great American Beer Festival.
- `trademark`: Great American Beer Festival and GABF are trademarks of the Brewers Association, used here only to name the competition.
