## How it is measured

Share is a family's medals divided by all medals in the year. Style families are this site's grouping of the official categories, not an official one.

## What it does not say

Medal share follows the category list as much as brewing. Each category awards up to three medals, so a family's share rises when the competition adds categories in it.
