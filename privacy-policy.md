# privacy-policy

## no-data-is-collected

- This project does not request, access, or store identifying information or configuration from your device beyond the browser preferences described below.
- This project does not collect usage statistics or use analytics or tracking cookies.
- This project does not sell or share personal information.

## browser-preferences

The site stores your theme and filter preferences locally in your browser. These preferences are not sent to the project maintainers. Filters can also appear in the page URL when you share a view. You can remove saved preferences by clearing the site's browser storage.

## hosting-and-external-links

The published site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting services may process technical information when serving pages under their own privacy policies. Links to brewery websites and other external resources take you to services governed by their own policies.

## scope

This policy applies to the public dashboard and the downloadable standalone dashboard. Information you choose to submit through GitHub issues, pull requests, or the local review feedback tool is outside the dashboard's no-collection statement.
