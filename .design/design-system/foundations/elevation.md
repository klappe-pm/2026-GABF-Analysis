# Elevation

## Purpose

There is no elevation. Depth is shown by tone and rules only.

## Rules

- Header bands and table headers sit on the ground tone.
- The Filters sheet and the estimate note are bounded by rules, not shadow.
- Layers order sticky table headers, the page header, and dialogs; they carry no visual depth.
- The backdrop behind a dialog is the ink color at reduced opacity.

## Do and do not

- Never use box shadow, text shadow, drop shadow, blur, or glass.
