# Data visualization

## Purpose

Charts are read like figures on a form: ruled, labelled, and backed by a table of the same numbers.

## Color

- Series one to five are graphite, judging green, iron gall, sepia, and pencil, in that order.
- Gold, silver, and bronze are used only when a series is a medal type.
- No default library palette.

## Marks

- Each series has its own mark shape, so color is never the only code: square, triangle, diamond, then hollow square and hollow triangle.
- Bars and boxes are square cornered.

## Frame

- Gridlines are hairline rules.
- No shadows in the plot or the tooltip.
- No animation.
- Every chart has a title that names the measure and span, a legend of drawn marks, and a table view.

## Do and do not

- Label directly where you can.
- Never lead a page with a row of equal tiles of single numbers over a grid of charts.
