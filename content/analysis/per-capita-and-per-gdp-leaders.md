## How it is measured

Each medal counts against its own year's Census population and real GDP. {{latest_year}} medals use {{data_year}} figures, the latest published. The geography dashboard adds Census regions, Federal Reserve districts, and demographic controls.

## What it does not say

A rate shows where medals land, not why. The record has no entry counts by state, so a high rate can mean more entries, better results, or both.
